Screening

13 Rooms — a site-specific exhibition

A dark night.
A shuttered inn beside the market.
Thirteen coffin-like rooms.
The film's virtual world, made real.▮

Coming Soon — 2027

In a dark corridor, monitor light spills from every open door. You choose the room you will enter — the world you will enter. When the film ends, open the old refrigerator in your room, take one cold can from the market supermarket, and come up to the rooftop.

Everyone who leaves feedback on this site is invited to the screening — seats are limited, so invitations work as free priority reservations; once invitation seats run out, we open participants-only sessions and an online screening. Those whose feedback is adopted come with the e-book in hand. Dates and reservations will be announced here.
