Revenue Ledger

Distribution and profit, too, are part of the work

Every won this work earns is published here. A film about the expansion and extinction of the AI age has no reason to hide how it makes money.

And one promise — a share of net profit, after production costs, goes to the people whose feedback was adopted into the work (the Contributing Writers). It may be a very small share. It might be pennies. Even so, we believe a world made together should share its earnings together. The exact ratio and method will be published on this page with the first settlement.
