Distribution and profit, too, are part of the work

Every cost this work incurs and every won it earns is published here. A film about the expansion and extinction of the AI age has no reason to hide how it spends and makes money.

And a promise to participants — if your feedback is adopted (Contributing Writers), you receive a copy of the e-book and an invitation to an offline screening; everyone who leaves feedback receives a screening invitation. It may not be much — but a world made together should be watched together.
